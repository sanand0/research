Metadata: No effect of PIT tagging method on survival or body condition in a northern population of Black-capped Chickadees (Poecile atricapilus)Authors: Jonathan J Farr, Elene Haave-Audet, Peter R Thompson, Kimberley J MathotCorresponding author: Jonathan J Farr, jfarr@ualberta.caData file: NORFID.csvDescription: Survival data and body mass data for Black-capped Chickadees of all three PIT tag treatments (control, leg band, implant) based on mist net recapturesVariables:ID: Unique chickadee identifierPIT: Passive integrated transponder treatment applied (A = control, B = leg band, C = implant)Event: Time of death (not recaptured in mist nets) in yearsCensored: If time of death was observed, censored = 1.
