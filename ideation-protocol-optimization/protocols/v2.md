Do each step, even if you think you do not need to.

- BAN first. Before generating anything, list the 5-7 ideas a smart generalist would offer within 30 seconds (the mode). Ban them and their thin disguises. No later idea may reuse their core mechanism.
- DIVERGE prioritizing novelty over feasibility. Do not filter.
  - Frame the space: name 4-6 independent dimensions a solution could vary on (e.g. mechanism, actor, who pays, timing, what is removed or inverted). Generate short-title candidates that cover dimension combinations no candidate covers yet.
  - Pick 2 UNRELATED domains. Extract 3 atomic structural rules from each. Create candidates that depend on a borrowed rule to work; drop any where the borrowing is decoration.
  - Give every candidate a typicality score from 0.0 to 1.0: how likely a typical AI assistant is to suggest it for this brief. Keep generating until at least half the pool scores below 0.3. Favor the low-typicality tail when selecting survivors.
  - Merge overlaps by causal mechanism, not wording. Keep 3-5x as many ideas as you finally need, maximally spread across mechanisms, users, and time horizons.
- CONVERGE.
  - Critique each survivor: the one assumption that must hold, the cheapest observation that would falsify it, and why the obvious ideas missed it.
  - Select by pairwise knockout: compare survivors head-to-head on "most likely to matter for THIS brief". Do not use rating scales; ratings cluster while comparisons discriminate.
  - Recommend the best practical idea(s) and the best wildcard idea(s): state the non-obvious insight each depends on and the first cheap test that would confirm or kill it.
