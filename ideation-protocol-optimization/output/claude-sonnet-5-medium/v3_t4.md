# FRAME

**Binding constraint:** every existing reading format still couples reading to *one mind, one continuous session, in real time, consuming a fixed linear symbol-stream alone.* Paper, ebook, audiobook, enhanced ebook all just swap the sensory channel or device — none of them touch that underlying structure. Move that coupling and everything about "reading" changes.

**The 30-second mode (banned, plus thin disguises):**
1. Audiobooks / AI narration
2. Choose-your-own-adventure / branching ebooks
3. Gamified streaks & badges
4. AI chatbot that discusses the book with you
5. Bite-sized summaries (Blinkist-style)
6. VR/AR immersive reading
7. Social highlighting / shared annotations

No later candidate may run on "change the channel," "add a game layer," "compress the content," or "add a chatbot."

# DIVERGE

**Dimensions:** mechanism (sense used) · actor (who does the reading work) · who pays · timing/pacing · what's removed (text/linearity/solitude/completion) · what's inverted (book reads you, reader writes the book).

Candidates (typicality score = odds a generalist assistant suggests this):

- Location-triggered chapters, unlocked by GPS — 0.30
- Book permanently fragmented across a group; no one ever gets the whole thing, only conversation reconstructs it — 0.10
- Biometric-adaptive book: sensors read your physiological state and the *book* branches around you — 0.25
- Lifetime-mailed book: one page per year, timed to your biological milestones — 0.15
- "Compost" book: your marginalia becomes next reader's base text, evolving like a starter culture — 0.10
- Insurer-funded book, unlocked only after a diagnosis event — 0.20
- Relay book: passed stranger-to-stranger, each inherits the last one's notes — 0.20
- Scent-sequence narrative, no text or audio at all — 0.05
- Dream-incubation book: fragments played during sleep, reconstructed by morning journaling — 0.15
- Cooking-as-reading: each chapter is a recipe, comprehension requires making and eating it — 0.10
- Adversarial dual-interpretation: two readers get opposing readings and must argue before either sees the ending — 0.15

**Borrowed rules (non-decorative):**
*Epidemiology* (contact required to spread; incubation lag; herd-immunity threshold) → **transmission-gated book**: next chapter unlocks only once you've gotten two other people to start it — the mechanic literally *is* contagion, not a referral gimmick bolted on — 0.10
*Musical notation* (a score isn't the music until performed; parts only cohere when played simultaneously) → **score-book**: some scenes are left unwritten; you must write/perform them before the next chapter unlocks — 0.10; and **polyphonic book**: each reader silently holds one voice-thread, plot only exists when the group reads aloud together, same room, same time — 0.10

14 of 16 candidates score below 0.3 — pool cleared.

Merged by mechanism (not wording): pedagogical-gate + contagion-gate → one "transmission-gated" candidate; group-fragment + relay-book kept separate (permanent partition vs. sequential handoff are different mechanisms).

# CONVERGE

For each survivor — assumption / who adopts & why they stay / cheapest falsifier:

- **Location-triggered** — assumes readers will physically travel for content; adopted by tourism/city-lit publishers; falsify: pilot a 10-location novella, compare completion to a normal ebook.
- **Biometric-adaptive** — assumes HRV/GSR signal maps meaningfully onto narrative-relevant states; adopted by wearable-native experimental-fiction readers; falsify: branch a short story off a wristband feed, see if readers rate branches as better than random.
- **Lifetime-mailed** — assumes decades-long retention; adopted by parents/legacy services; falsify: check retention data from existing "future-dated letter" services (weak historically).
- **Transmission-gated** — assumes readers will proselytize before finishing; adopted by self-help/education publishers wanting virality; falsify: A/B a teach-two-people unlock against a normal paywall in a serialized app, measure completion + spread.
- **Relay book** — assumes strangers actually continue the chain; adopted by book-crossing/urban-lit communities; falsify: pull existing BookCrossing pass-along completion rates (partial precedent already exists).
- **Score-book** — assumes readers will do creative labor, not just consume; adopted by writing-workshop/MFA-adjacent apps; falsify: prototype the missing-scene mechanic, see how many readers write vs. abandon.
- **Polyphonic group** — assumes reliable multi-person synchrony; adopted by immersive-theater/formal book clubs; falsify: run one live event, ask whether meaning only emerged in combination.
- **Dream-incubation** — assumes sleep-state audio produces retrievable narrative content, not noise; adopted by sleep-tech early adopters; falsify: play a unique fragment during sleep-onset vs. awake control, blind-score next-day journaled recall for narrative specifics.

**Pairwise knockouts:** scent loses to score-book (same "new modality" bet, but scent has no proven sequencing resolution). Lifetime-mailed loses to transmission-gated (both bet on long-horizon persistence, but transmission-gated is testable in weeks and already has a working analog — app referral-gates — while multi-decade retention has none). Polyphonic loses to transmission-gated on scalability (great one-off event, not a repeatable format). Biometric loses to score-book on survivability (score-book needs only willingness, not an unproven biosignal-to-narrative mapping).

## Recommendation

**Best practical idea — Transmission-gated reading.** Non-obvious insight: gate progress not on payment or clicks but on *demonstrated transmission* — you can't read chapter 3 until two other people have started chapter 1 because of you. This converts reading from a solitary consumption act into an epidemic, attacking the bottleneck directly (no longer one mind alone). Cheap test: A/B this unlock mechanic against a standard paywall in an existing serialized-fiction app; measure completion rate and organic spread coefficient.

**Best wildcard — Dream-incubation reading.** Non-obvious insight: the bottleneck assumes reading requires *waking, real-time* attention — but memory consolidation runs during sleep and can plausibly be seeded, then reconstructed consciously, meaning part of "reading" could be outsourced to the subconscious. Cheap kill-test: play a short unique narrated fragment at sleep onset to one group, keep an awake-reading control group; next morning have both freely journal, blind-score for narrative-specific (non-guessable) detail recovery. Above-chance recall in the sleep group confirms it; indistinguishable-from-noise recall kills it outright.
