Which approach to trust

Use a pose model for scoring, and a vision-language model only for commentary. The four pose models agree on every rep in three of four sessions (60 / 60 / 60). In the backlit fourth they agree on 50, and only YOLO double-counts. RTMPose-x is the best single model: it agrees most with the others, shakes least among the accurate ones, and runs at ~60 ms/frame. The ensemble (median of all four) is the safest default.

Gemini 4 Argon finds 90–100% of the reps and writes genuinely useful coaching — it spotted the piked top and the "worming" ascent. But its numbers are flat and miscalibrated, and it can't draw proof. Gemini 3.8 Flash missed 35 seconds of real push-ups.

No hand-labelled keypoints exist, so "accuracy" here means agreement with the other models, frame-to-frame steadiness, rep counts against an adjudicated set (reps ≥ 3 of 4 pose models agree on, plus eyeballed rulings on every dispute), and the visual audit below. Click a row to view that approach.

Reading the overlay

Arc at the elbow = elbow angle. Dashed line = a perfectly straight body from shoulder to ankle; the short line from the hip shows how far the hips sag or pike. Dotted line past the shoulder = where a neutral head would be. Bottom dotted line = floor (wrists to toes), with the shoulder's height above it in arm-lengths. Green / amber / red = good / borderline / poor by the scoring thresholds.
